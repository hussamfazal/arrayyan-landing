Why Ar-Rayyan

The moment your mind asks “two or three?” shouldn’t take you away.

Ar-Rayyan was made for a familiar moment: losing count in salah. Its depth-aware tracker recognizes each prostration and keeps count in real time, so you can return your attention to the prayer itself.

Awareness, in three simple steps.

No wearables to configure. No screen to touch while you pray. Just open, place, and begin.

01 / PLACE◡

Place your phone

Set it screen-up in the centre of your prayer mat and choose the number of rak’ahs.

02 / PRAY⌁

Pray naturally

Ar-Rayyan’s sensors recognize each sujood as you move naturally through prayer.

03 / TRUST✦

Let Ar-Rayyan count

Two sujoods complete a rak’ah. Gentle on-screen feedback keeps track without interruption.
